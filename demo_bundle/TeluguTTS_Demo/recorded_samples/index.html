Telugu text-to-speech: prototype samples

Pre-generated audio from our fine-tuned model (Kyutai Pocket TTS, 24-layer, trained on open Telugu speech data). Press play on any line.

What this is. A research prototype trained in one day on about 145 hours of open Telugu data (SYSPIN studio recordings and Rasa). The audio below was generated ahead of time by the model, one sentence at a time, in two reference voices (a male and a female studio speaker).
Where it stands. Our read-back test (a speech recogniser listening to the output) found the generated speech about as recognisable as real recordings; a Telugu-speaking reviewer judged it very good. These are early, small-sample results.
Not yet covered. Numbers and dates, English words in Latin script, and long paragraphs (the model speaks one sentence at a time). Speed-ups for phones and laptops are future work. The voices come from open-dataset speakers; voice-consent and licensing questions are still being settled, so please do not circulate the audio.
