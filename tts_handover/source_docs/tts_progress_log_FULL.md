## 1. Context-restoration brief

**What the project is.** The company wants **speech technology for Hindi, Telugu and English**: STT (speech-to-text) and TTS (text-to-speech). The senior's earlier requirement (relayed by the owner): "Telugu gets its own TTS and STT, and Hindi and English too", i.e. **separate monolingual models per language**. Commercial use, so every dataset and base-model licence must allow it. Edge deployment (phones and laptops) was the original aim [targets still unconfirmed].

**Why TTS now.** The STT 3-week plan was approved in spirit by the senior ("loved our work"), but **urgent constraints mean the team must start TTS immediately** (owner, 2026-10-14). STT is paused, not cancelled; see `../stt/progress.md`.

**Known constraints (from the STT work, may apply to TTS):** compute is **rented on AWS** (8x A100 40 GB were mentioned, but the plan is to rent small GPUs in bursts; p4d.24xlarge costs about $21.96/h, g5 A10G instances $1.01-1.21/h); the **budget is very tight**; open data only; evidence is labelled and logged.

**What we know about TTS so far** (details in `research_notes/carried_over_from_stt_research.md`; none of it re-checked by us):
- **Kyutai Pocket TTS** is the open starting point we know: ~100M-class, CPU-friendly, MIT code, CC BY 4.0 gated weights; **training code released Aug 2026** with a language fine-tune recipe and a depth-distillation recipe; Kyutai's guidance is 100 h minimum, 200 h "speaks", 2,000 h "sounds good".
- **Prasad Vittaldev's Telugu TTS** (124 h teacher fine-tune, then a 6-layer ~110M single-voice student on 53 h) shows the recipe works at small scale, but its **data provenance is unclear and quality was never measured** (no WER/MOS).
- Candidate TTS datasets: **SYSPIN** (Hindi ~119 h, Telugu ~115 h; licence notes conflict), **Rasa** (Hindi ~51 h, Telugu ~52 h; CC BY 4.0), **IndicTTS** (licence text unread), **IndicVoices-R** (licence conflict).
- Community Hindi Pocket TTS builds exist (932 h, 647 h, ~90 h).
- **The senior's belief** that "swapping Telugu data into Kyutai" gives a PoC in an hour or two is **plausible only for the GPU part of a teacher fine-tune**; the data, alignment, tokenizer fixes and listening tests take days [E].

**Things we do not know:** see Section 6 (open questions): which languages first, how many voices, voice cloning or not, target devices, quality bar and how it is judged, deadline, budget, and whether consented studio recordings are possible.
