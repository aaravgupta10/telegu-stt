Method: pages opened and their visible text read in the Claude desktop built-in browser pane while the owner was signed in to Hugging Face. **No gate was accepted and nothing was clicked; no files were downloaded.** Text below is a condensed capture (paraphrased or short excerpts), not a full copy. ## AI4Bharat model repos (gate = Hugging Face default prompt; licence tag mit)
- `ai4bharat/indicconformer_stt_hi_hybrid_ctc_rnnt_large` and the `_te_` equivalent: licence tag **mit**. Gate text visible: "You need to agree to share your contact information to access this model ... publicly accessible, but you have to accept the conditions to access its files ... By agreeing you accept to share your contact information (email and username) with the repository authors." Button: "Agree and access repository". Architecture per card: conformer-Large encoder **120M params, 17 conformer blocks, model dim 512, hybrid CTC-RNNT decoder**; input 16 kHz mono wav. Needs **AI4Bharat's NeMo fork** (`github.com/AI4Bharat/NeMo`, branch `nemo-v2`, `reinstall.sh`). Hindi repo: 12 likes, 783 downloads last month. Telugu: 10 likes, 212 downloads. Both are in the "IndicConformer" collection (23 items, updated Mar 2).
- `ai4bharat/indic-conformer-600m-multilingual`: licence tag **mit**; same default gate text. Card says IndicConformer is released under the MIT license. 600M, hybrid CTC+RNNT, 22 languages including Telugu and Hindi; ONNX + `custom_code`. **New banner (not in earlier notes): "Indic-Transcribe is now available. It supports 25 Indian languages and has better transcription quality compared to IndicConformer. We recommend using Indic-Transcribe."** Indic-Transcribe is the Bodhan model whose licence the audit flagged as restrictive (report Section 5). 499,859 downloads last month; 223 likes.

## ARTPARK-IISc model repos (gate = default prompt; licence tag mit)
- `ARTPARK-IISc/SraVaani-1.0`: licence tag **mit**; default contact-sharing gate. ~430M FastConformer, hybrid TDT-CTC, FP16 (~900 MB). Pretrained from scratch on 31,255 h of Vaani (105 languages), then audio-image alignment on 11M pairs, then fine-tuned on ~31,270 h transcribed speech in 65 languages "using a combination of the Vaani dataset and **multiple open-source speech datasets**" (**which ones is not stated on the card**). Hindi, Telugu and English supported. `custom_code`; the usage example needs an access token. arXiv 2608.08235.
- `ARTPARK-IISc/Vaani-FastConformer-Telugu`: licence tag **mit**; default gate. NeMo `EncDecRNNTBPEModel`. "Dataset used to train: ARTPARK-IISc/Vaani". Part of the "Vaani-FastConformer" collection (5 items, updated Aug 7). 22 downloads last month. The card gives no parameter count, WER or training hours.

## Datasets
- `ai4bharat/IndicVoices`: licence tag **cc-by-4.0**. **No gate box was visible on the dataset page in this session** (unknown whether the account already has access or the page shows it elsewhere). Columns (README YAML): `audio_filepath, text, duration, lang, samples, verbatim, normalized, speaker_id, scenario, task_name, gender, age_group, job_type, qualification, area, district, state, occupation, verification_report, unsanitized_verbatim, unsanitized_normalized`. **Rows: hindi 451k (train 445,160; valid 5,530); telugu 273k (train ~269k; valid ~3.3k).** `samples` = duration x 16,000 (16 kHz). **In the visible Assamese rows, `unsanitized_normalized` keeps English words as `native [english]` pairs** (e.g. "ডেলিভাৰী [delivery]"), while `text`, `verbatim` and `normalized` do not. Noise tags such as `[uhh]` and `[horn]` appear in the `unsanitized_*` fields. **Hindi and Telugu rows could NOT be viewed** (viewer timeout / "not available for this split"), so whether Hindi and Telugu behave the same is unverified. The viewer does not show hour totals.
- `ai4bharat/Kathbath`: licence tag **cc-by-4.0**; gate text = default contact-sharing prompt. Card body: "We do not own any of the raw text used in creating this dataset. The text data comes from the IndicCorp dataset which is a crawl of publicly available websites ... We license the actual packaging of all this data under the Creative Commons CC0 license". So the tag says CC-BY and the body says CC0 packaging. 1,684 h, 12 languages, 1,218 contributors.
- `ARTPARK-IISc/Vaani`: licence tag **cc-by-4.0**; arXiv 2603.28714; 106 subsets; English 272k rows. Hindi and Telugu row counts did not render in the text capture. **No gate box text was visible in this session** (same caveat as IndicVoices). - Licence tag **cc-by-4.0**. Gate text on the page (before accepting): "Numo Indic Speech is free to use under CC BY 4.0, including commercially. Share a few details to get access. - Card: "quality-filtered, crowdsourced speech corpus collected by Poseidon AI, Inc.", **353,349 recordings, 3,845.5 h** across Bengali, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu; **scripted, single-speaker read speech**. Per language: **Hindi 101,450 recordings / 1,314.6 h; Telugu 132,488 / 1,224.2 h**; Bengali 57,628 / 745.1 h; Tamil 61,783 / 561.6 h. Full-resolution WAV in Parquet; per-recording WER/CER and synthetic-speech suspicion score; SHA-256 per audio object; speaker ID, age, gender, country, region when available; one split per language configuration.
- Card claim: "The corpus retains code-switching and commonly used English terms present in the original reading prompts." **How English words are written (Latin or native script) is not stated in the part read.**
- Privacy notice (last updated August 26, 2026): contributors may provide "Audio and Video Recordings" and "Content you submit may be licensed to third party AI companies who may use it for purposes including voice and visual AI model training." Payment info collected includes "your blockchain wallet address" (contributors are compensated). So contributors are told their recordings may be licensed to third-party AI companies; the notice does not by itself show they consented to an open CC BY release. Only the first ~4,500 characters of the notice were read.
